Net force = (mud density × outer volume) − (pipe material weight, 950 kg/m³ HDPE) − (internal fill weight). The loop pipe is closed and pre-filled with water; an open tremie floods with mud. Minimum mass is corrected for the loop weight's own buoyancy in mud (7850 kg/m³ mild steel), scaled by the safety factor, then rounded up to whole 40kg units. The safety factor is a margin for wall friction, deviation and other immeasurable factors, not a substitute for site-specific engineering judgement. Estimates for planning only; confirm against project and supplier specifications before installation.
